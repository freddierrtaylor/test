# AFTERSHIFT: concept

An automotive discovery and collection game set in a fictionalised Bahrain and the Gulf. The full generation prompt is in [`aftershift-prompt.md`](aftershift-prompt.md). This page is the short version of the concept, with some first ideas for places and discoveries.

## The pitch

The player starts with an inexpensive car, a modest workshop and very little money. They explore a world inspired by Bahrain, uncover rare vehicles, meet local collectors, discover hidden garages, buy and restore cars, and work their way into the region's exclusive motorsport scene.

The game should feel distinctly Middle Eastern rather than a generic racing game with a desert texture added.

## The four pillars

| Pillar | What it is | What the player does there |
|---|---|---|
| **The circuit** | A motorsport complex inspired by Bahrain International Circuit | Amateur time trials, then track days, club races, night races, drag nights and endurance events |
| **The city** | A fictionalised city inspired by Manama, with illuminated towers, waterfront boulevards, underground car parks, dealerships, cafés and late-night meets | Meet people, pick up leads, buy and sell, cruise at sunset and at night |
| **The desert** | Long open roads, interchanges, industrial compounds, remote workshops and hidden stashes | Follow leads, find cars and parts, take on desert driving challenges |
| **The Gulf car scene** | Collector garages, luxury SUVs, supercars, modified cars, drag racing and specialist workshops | Build a reputation and earn invitations into each community |

## Places to start from

All the names below are placeholders. Each place is fictional and only inspired by a real one.

- **The circuit complex** (inspired by Sakhir): the GP layout and the shorter inner loop, a pit lane and paddock, the tower, grandstands, a drag strip, floodlights for night racing, and a desert ring road around the site.
- **The bay district** (inspired by Bahrain Bay and the Seef waterfront): glass towers, hotels, showrooms and multi-level underground parking.
- **The old quarter** (inspired by Muharraq): coral-stone houses with wind towers and narrow lanes. Old family garages here hide forgotten cars.
- **The industrial estate** (inspired by Sitra and the port areas): workshops, parts yards, body shops and tuning shops, and the best place to hear rumours.
- **The oil field desert** (inspired by Awali and the southern desert): nodding pumps, old company compounds, pipelines and service roads. Long-closed sheds and staff garages hold the rarest finds.
- **The causeway** (inspired by the King Fahd Causeway): a long sea crossing used for highway runs and cross-border collector deals.
- **The island coast** (inspired by the south-west coast and its islands): coastal roads, beach clubs, salt-air rust and boat-yard barns.

## Example discoveries

These show the "cars in context" rule in practice.

- A dusty 1970s American saloon, still on its original company registration, in a sealed shed at an old oil-company compound. The lead comes from a retired engineer at a café.
- A pioneering local drag car, half-dismantled behind a closed tuning shop. To unlock it, the player has to win a drag night and earn the owner's family's trust.
- A limited-production supercar that only appears at an invitation-only collector gathering. The owner will trade it only for a properly restored classic.
- A retired circuit race car from an early club season, found by completing that era's lap record in a time-trial challenge.
- A classic 4x4 abandoned at a desert camp, which has to be recovered across sand before it can be restored.

## Tone and authenticity

- Show ordinary life alongside luxury. Daily-driver saloons, pickups, delivery vans and taxis share the road with supercars.
- Use Arabic and English signage, right-hand traffic, regional road furniture and architecture, and NPCs whose clothing suits the place.
- Car culture here is social. It happens at cafés, family garages, weekend meets and late nights out.
- Avoid stereotypes. No caricatured wealth and no "exotic desert" clichés.
- The world is fictionalised. Real places are only visual references, and the map does not claim to be an exact copy of Bahrain.
- Use original or properly licensed vehicles, logos and brands.

## How the existing prototype fits

The [Oasis Circuit Sim](../index.html) in this repo is a working prototype of AFTERSHIFT's **circuit** pillar. It has a BIC-inspired track with elevation, pits, time trials with medals, races against AI, night lighting, replays, and per-car physics and sound. The parts most worth carrying forward are:

- **Time trials with medal times**: the first rung on the circuit career ladder.
- **Per-car physics** (FWD understeer, RWD oversteer, turbo lag, tyre wear): a base for making a neglected car feel different from a restored one.
- **The secret car unlock** (Delta S4, F2004): an early taste of the "rare car behind a challenge" discovery loop.
